Privacy & demo policy.

Atlas Digital Studio is a fictional educational website for practicing marketing operations. It is not a real service provider.

What this website does

It demonstrates navigation, a service-page buyer journey and a simulated contact form. There are no customers, offers, bookings, quotations or actual sales operations behind this website.

Form data

The current inquiry form processes the content locally in your browser to display a sample JSON event. It does not post your form values to our servers, to an email inbox, to Tally, to n8n or to HubSpot. Avoid entering real personal information. Sample data uses reserved, non-deliverable .invalid addresses.

Campaign information

To demonstrate attribution, the local JavaScript reads only whitelisted UTM parameters (source, medium, campaign, term and content) and the landing page path. It remembers the first known campaign in the current browser tab session and updates the latest tagged visit. It does not set cross-session marketing cookies or identify visitors. Browser session storage is cleared when the browsing session ends.

Analytics and third parties

GA4, GTM, Tally and email automation are not connected in this initial demo. No third-party marketing analytics scripts or external fonts are loaded by the project files. When those integrations are added, this notice and the consent controls must be reviewed and updated before collecting real data.

Public demonstration caveat

Once deployed publicly, the host may process standard technical request logs under its own terms. The site should not be used to solicit or collect real inquiries until its purpose, integrations and privacy obligations have been properly reviewed.

Scope and reuse

All company details, performance claims and opportunities in this lab are fictional. Reuse the technical patterns for client work only with appropriate authorization, a tailored privacy notice, security review, and client-owned accounts.
